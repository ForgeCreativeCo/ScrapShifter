# Scrap Shifter (working title)

Transforming-vehicle battle game, Joe's browser prototype. This file carries the context from the original design chat.

## Concept

- Large-map, third-person 3D battle game, playable in the browser.
- Single-player first, multiplayer later.
- Transforming vehicles with four modes:
  - **Buggy**: fast, light gun, exposed.
  - **Artillery**: stabilizer legs plant and lift the wheels, long barrel extends, side armor folds up. Stationary, long range, heaviest shell.
  - **Mech**: stands upright. Arm cannon fires flat and fast, left arm carries a shield, shield bash and running ram for close-range smashing.
  - **Gyro**: the wheels swing out flat as rotors and the rig flies; drops heavy bombs. Hit-and-run strikes, limited by heat, fragile in the air.
- Destructible environment is a core hook.
- Visual style: junkyard / war-torn. Rusty, mismatched, scrap-built panels. Heavy, non-cartoony explosions.
- **Premise: the swarm eats towns.** "The Scrap" is a self-building machine swarm that strips towns for material and builds more of itself; crawlers and bikes are its units. The player is a salvager in a rig built from swarm parts. Clean modern town vs. rusty scrap machines is the visual identity.
- **Core tension: rubble feeds the swarm.** Destroying buildings near a living foundry makes it grow and spawn faster; fire denies the material. So the player chooses: shell it (fast, feeds the others), breach it with the mech (exposed), or burn it out (slow).
- **Sandbox mode:** no objective; destruction raises a wanted level (1–5 stars). Low levels send crawlers and bikes; from 3 stars, rival rigs (machines like the player's, all four modes) hunt you.
- **Race mode:** a point-to-point race against rival rigs whose finish line is a multi-level battle arena; last rig standing wins. Ammo only comes from crates on the track. Buggy only on the track (mech too in the arena), one RC bike launch. Gun towers beside the track shoot at every racer.
- **Run loop (roguelite):** briefing/garage → destroy the foundries → escort the evac convoy out → extract → debrief and spend salvage on rig upgrades. Multiplayer later: co-op squads (deterministic destruction supports it).

## Current prototype

three.js r128 from cdnjs, no build step. Open `index.html` directly in a browser (double-click works).

### Layout
`index.html` holds the page, CSS and HUD markup, then loads **classic scripts** from `js/` in this order. They share one global scope: no import/export, so ES modules aren't needed and `file://` keeps working. Top-level names must stay unique across files. A file's top-level code may only use things from earlier files, but functions can call anything at runtime.

`node serve.js` serves the folder at http://localhost:5173. Only needed for Claude's preview pane, which can't load `js/` from a `file://` page. `.claude/launch.json` has the `scrap-shifter` config for it. If a `js/` file fails to load, the status bar says so. Runtime errors also show there with file:line.

- Vehicle is built from simple parts. Each part has a pose per mode, and transforms are interpolated with a staggered delay.
- Procedural canvas textures for rust, hazard stripes, concrete, dirt, and smoke.
- Terrain: a heightfield (0.5 m samples, `gHt`/`gCol`) drawn as 32 m chunk meshes. Each chunk has three LOD index ranges in one index buffer (every sample / every 2nd / every 4th, by camera distance 72 m and 140 m, `terrainLod`); the chunk border stays full resolution and fans into the coarse interior, so there are no cracks. `editGround()` changes samples and rebuilds only the touched patch (about 1–2 ms per crater on the 360 m map). Normals come from the heightfield, so there are no seams. Explosions dig craters and scorch colors; rubble raises mounds. `gh()` is bilinear.
- Maps (`js/maps.js`): `test` is the 120 m field with the target gallery. `town` is the real battle map: a modern town at night, 448 m (`HALF_T` 224). Sky from `MAP.sky` (colors, sun/moon, hemisphere light). The ground is one painted 4096² texture across the whole map (asphalt with lane lines, sidewalks and curbs, zebra crossings, paved lots, lawns, meadow), so craters dent and scorch roads too. A 44 m street grid (16 m streets) covers ±162 m, 7×7 blocks (`TP.N` = 3 rings round the centre block; `TP.LINES`/`TP.EDGE` derive from it): central plaza with fountain; ring 1 downtown glass/concrete towers (5–8 floors) and mid-rises; ring 2 a mix of downtown and residential; ring 3 1–3 floor stucco/brick/concrete buildings around lawns (`props.lawns`). 86 buildings, about 4,600 pieces. Street trees, lamp posts, parked cars and a few concrete jersey barriers line the streets. Grassy wooded hills outside, a tree line at the edge, and a highway in from the west (start at x=-194 on z=22; start, extraction and convoy route are written relative to `HALF_T`). Countryside trees and rocks are scattered over the map square but never inside the town square or on the highway (`inTown`/`onHwy` in props.js).
- Night / neon look (`MAP.night`, town only): near-black sky with stars (`sky.stars`), dark cloud colour (`sky.cloudCol`), magenta haze in fog and horizon, a dim moon as the shadow light, stronger bloom (`sky.bloom`), `sky.exposure`. All the neon is unlit or emissive, so it adds no lights: modern facades get an emissive lit-window mask (`EM` canvas drawn next to the gloss mask) tinted per panel in the vertex shader from a hash of the instance position (`litPatch`: ~30% dark, most warm, some cyan/magenta/violet; scorched panels fade); roof-edge strips, shop sign bars and blade signs with glyphs are two unlit `TiledInst`s tied to wall panels (`p.neon`, hidden by `neonState` when the panel goes); lamp heads are unlit instance-coloured boxes (one colour per street segment); the light they throw on the ground is painted into a 1024² map (`paintTownGlow` → `townGlow`) used as the ground's emissive map, multiplied by the ground paint and by vertex colours (so craters and stains darken it). The only real light added is `rigLamp`, one point light carried ahead of the player rig. Neon colours come from `NEON` (townplan.js); each building has a `neon` colour index.
- Destructible targets break into physics-ish chunks.
- Effects: sprite fireballs, lingering smoke, dust surge, embers, slow-mo on big hits, trauma-based camera shake.
- Sound: recorded effects in `sfx/` (see `sfx/SOURCES.md` for what plays where) are packed into `js/sfxdata.js` and decoded when the AudioContext starts; until then, or if decoding fails, the synthesized versions play. World sounds arrive at the speed of sound, pan by camera direction and get muffled with distance (`spot`). Loops: artillery turret motor while the gun moves, crawler treads by distance and speed. Building collapses are the B explosion slowed down. The player engine, clanks, fire crackle, honks and pickups are still synthesized. Music and ambience per map (`MAP.audio`, `musicTick`): the town loops city ambience and alternates two cinematic tracks with a 25–45 s gap; both fade out under the briefing/garage overlay; N (or the Music button) toggles music, saved in localStorage.
- Buggy boost (drive.js `BOOST`, `boostK`): hold Shift while driving forward for 1.3× top speed (17 → 22 m/s) and 1.6× pull, with flames out of the back and the camera pulling back. It builds `TUNE.heatBoost` (20/s) on the shared `HEAT` gauge: at 100 it overheats (steam, siren) and boost is locked until 35. Steering gets wider with speed, so boosting through corners runs wide.
- Driving: WASD/arrows. Buggy is fast and can't turn in place; mech walks slowly and turns in place; artillery is planted (WASD flies the drone instead). Transforming bleeds off speed. Vehicle follows terrain slope, collides with scenery (`solids`) and targets.
- Mech walk: procedural gait driven by distance actually moved, so planted feet don't slide. Two-bone IK legs (knees bend forward, wheel knee guards ride the knee), body dips on each footfall and sways side to side, and the mech lurches forward mid-stride. Each footfall plays a thud, kicks up a dust ring and adds a small camera shake. Hold Shift to run: stride, step height and foot-down time blend from walk to run values by speed (run has a flight phase), footfalls hit harder, turning widens, and the camera pulls back.
- All gait values live in `TUNE` (defaults in `TUNE_DEF`). The Tune panel (T) edits them live and saves to localStorage; "Copy values" gives JSON to bake back into `TUNE_DEF`.
- Artillery spotter drone (js/drone.js, `DRONE`): when the legs plant, a scrap quadcopter lifts off the deck and the camera blends to a view behind and above it (fog pushed back, shadows follow its look point via `DR.focus`). WASD flies it (18 m/s), Q/E or right-drag turns, scroll sets altitude 16–50 m; it climbs over intact roofs (`roofAt`). Tethered to 130 m from the rig: pushed back past 90%, static on screen near the limit. Its red laser paints the point under the cursor, snaps onto walls/roofs and sticks to an enemy (`DR.tgt`, led by the shell's flight time). Switching to buggy or mech snaps the camera back at once and the drone flies home and docks during the transform. Crawlers fire 3-round flak bursts at it within 42 m with a clear line (accuracy falls with distance and drone speed, 10 per hit, 60 HP). A downed drone crashes, explodes small and rebuilds in 15 s; meanwhile the artillery aims with the cursor from the rig (red bracket marker). HUD: drone status line, warnings (rig under fire, hostiles near the rig, weak signal), a tag at the laser dot (range, flight time, LOCKED / OUT OF RANGE / TOO CLOSE) and a rig tag pinned to the screen edge when off screen. The hover sound is the recorded drone loop placed at the drone (synth buzz until it decodes).
- Artillery point defence (js/turrets.js, `MG`): two minigun turrets are vehicle parts tucked inside the hull in buggy/mech and slide out to the hull sides in artillery. Once planted, each picks the nearest living enemy within 40 m with a clear line (`fireLine`, rechecked every 0.3 s), slews (4.5 rad/s yaw, 3 pitch), pre-spins its six barrels while it has a line, and fires once laid: 18 dps × hit chance (falls with distance, a little with target speed), applied every 0.12 s via `damageEnemy`, with tracers, muzzle flash and dust on misses. Two guns stop a rusher bike 11–25 m out and kill a crawler in ~3 s. Sound: `sfx/minigun.mp3` steady part looped while any gun fires, wind-down tail when they stop.
- Artillery live aim (`aimTick`): the turret follows the laser (or cursor) continuously at 2.6 rad/s traverse, 1.6 rad/s elevation, all the way round. Default is the flat arc; hold Shift for the high arc (falls back to flat when too close to lob). Click or Space queues a shot (`AIM.pending`) that fires once the gun is laid (tight tolerance) and loaded. No shot inside 10 m (`ART_MIN`) or out of range. Muzzle 37 m/s gives about 140 m range on flat ground. `solveHull()` is shared with buggy/mech mouse aim and corrects for barrel length.
- Aiming: `predict()` steps a virtual shell with the same gravity as real shots (shared `shotHits`) to draw the arc and an impact ring sized to the blast radius (2.3 × power). Buggy/mech aim with the mouse: `cursorPoint()` raycasts targets and marches the heightfield under the cursor, `gunAim()` solves the low ballistic arc, converts it to hull space (slopes handled) and slews the gun within `TURRET` limits (buggy ±120°, mech arm ±34°; turn the mech for more). Left click fires (hold to repeat; artillery queues one shot per click), right-drag looks around. Buggy/mech show a fainter ring only. The camera sits behind the turret and looks partway toward the impact; fog is pushed back. Muzzle speed/power in `FIRE`, reload times in `RELOAD` (artillery 2.2s); the marker greys out while reloading.
- Combat (`// ---------- Combat` section): `explode()` calls `applyBlast()`, which damages anything within 2.3×power+1.2 m with linear falloff. Shells carry an `owner` so they never hit their shooter, and `shotHits()` checks enemies and the player. Player hull is 100 with armor per mode (`ARMOR`: buggy 1.25, artillery 0.7, mech 0.85) and 1.5× damage mid-transform. It repairs 5/s after 5 s without damage. Death wrecks the vehicle (burning, slumped, controls locked); in a mission that ends the run after 2.5 s. R restarts the run (resets enemies, shells, kills, the field and the foundries).
- Enemy strength (combat.js): `FOE_HP` and `FOE_DMG` (both 0.25) scale every enemy's health and all damage enemies deal: shells, bike charges and wreck explosions (`foeK()` in `applyBlast`/`truckBlast`; the player's own blasts stay full), flak, rig bash. Numbers below are before scaling.
- Enemy crawlers: low tracked scrap vehicles (red hull, plow blade, red sensor lamp). Without a mission (free play), up to `ENEMY_MAX`=2 respawn 8 s apart on the map ring (`spawnPoint`: the nearest to the player of the first 4 valid ring points); in missions they come from foundries (js/swarm.js) and, after the foundries fall, a counter-attack from the ring. They patrol waypoints and engage inside 48 m: close in, back off under 14 m, circle-strafe in the 14–26 m band. The turret leads the player imperfectly (`e.lead`), solves the low arc and fires when laid (2.4–3.4 s reload). The lamp brightens just before a shot. Each enemy shell gets a pulsing red warning ring at its predicted impact (`simShell`). 60 HP: buggy shots take 2, artillery 1. HUD shows hull, kills and floating enemy health bars.
- Scrap-bike rushers (`makeBike`/`bikeTick` in combat.js): one at a time, 14–20 s apart, first after ~20 s. They weave in at 11 m/s and detonate within 2.6 m (power 1.6 charge), with the beacon and beep speeding up as they close. 30 HP, so one buggy shot kills one, and shooting it sets the charge off (chain-kills nearby crawlers). Enemies share `killEnemy(e,credit)` and per-type hit sizes (`e.r`, `e.hh`, `e.maxHp`, `e.barY`).
- Mech close-range role (mech.js/combat.js): F = shield bash (`SMASH_*`): wind-up, lunge, swing of the `ARM` parts about the shoulder. One-shots crawlers (knockback + stun), smashes targets and walls, adds 12 heat. The shield blocks 75% of blasts from the front arc (`shieldBlocks`). A running mech rams: shatters targets, smashes walls (`RAM_STRUCT`) and shoves/damages crawlers (`RAM_DMG`).
- Mech heat (`HEAT`, mech.js): running builds heat, overheating at 100 vents steam and locks run + bash until it drops to 35. A siren (`sfx/siren.mp3`, looped) sounds while overheated in mech or gyro mode. Rates are in the Tune panel.
- Structures (js/structures.js, layout in `LAYOUT` in world.js): wall segments and sheds with swap states intact → damaged (≤50% HP) → rubble. Deterministic: seeded shapes, HP thresholds, rubble raises a terrain `mound`. They block movement (`obbPush`), shells (`pieceHitShot`) and crawler fire, and take blast/bash/ram damage. An indestructible instanced scrap perimeter at ±58.9 hides the map edge.
- Buildings (js/buildings.js, `MAP.buildings`: x, z, rot, mx×mz 4 m modules, floors, style brick/concrete/corr): one wall-panel piece per module per floor per side, plus a slab per level. Ground floor is 5.6 m tall (workshop bays, so the mech fits), upper floors 3.4 m. Only ground-floor panels block movement. Panels go intact → damaged (jagged strips) → gone (ground floor leaves debris); HP 70 ground / 50 upper, slabs 100, damage × `TUNE.bldDmg` (default 2.6). When `TUNE.collapse` (default 60%) of a floor's panels are gone, that floor and everything above collapse (`collapseFrom`). A full collapse leaves a rubble heap mound and rubble meshes; a partial one cracks the slab below. Intact panels and slabs are instances in a few `InstancedMesh`es (hidden by zero-scale matrix); damaged visuals are created on demand. Pieces have `y0` (base height) and `blocks`. All piece queries go through the 8 m spatial grid (`piecesNear`).
- Enemy navigation (js/nav.js): a 2 m grid where blocked = map edge + scenery + blocking pieces inflated by 1.7 m (crawler hull). Destroyed or reset pieces mark their area dirty (`navDirty`), and `navFlush` re-checks only those cells, so breaches (2+ panels) and collapses open routes immediately. 3 m alleys stay closed to crawlers. `findPath` is weighted A* (×1.5, 8-way, no corner cutting, string-pulled), ~1–3 ms typical; at most 2 searches per frame (`navBudget`). `navWant(e,gx,gz)` goes straight when `navLine` is clear, else follows a cached path (re-planned every ~1.5 s or when the goal moves); partial paths end with a straight push. `navStuck` triggers a reverse-and-replan. A flood fill from the map start (`navReachable`) keeps spawns and roam targets out of sealed pockets.
- Crawler AI with nav: out of range they hunt (roam to reachable points within 30 m of the player). Engaged, they path in until within 26 m **with line of fire** (`fireLine`, a 3D check through holes and over low walls), and only fire with it. In the 14–26 m band they strafe and flip direction at walls. Bikes weave in the open and follow streets otherwise.
- Modern facade styles (`stucco`, `mbrick`, `mconc`, `mglass`) with framed windows, glass lobbies and storefronts (`shop0..2` awning colors, one per building). Facade materials are created lazily (`facadeM` proxy), so a map only builds what it uses. The old shanty styles (`brick`, `concrete`, `corr`) remain for the test field.
- The camera pulls in when a building piece sits between the vehicle and the camera (`camPull`). Cursor aim checks building panels by marching the spatial grid instead of raycasting instances.
- Parked cars (js/cars.js) are pieces (kind 'car', `blocks:false`, `dmgMul` 1.6, 70 HP). Intact → damaged (darker, sagging, steaming) → wrecked (charred, flattened, tires gone). A wreck explodes 0.1–0.45 s later (`CAR_BOOM` power 1.8), which wrecks the next car in a bumper-to-bumper row, so rows chain. It burns ~14 s, then smolders. Husks still block movement via the car's `solids` circles but no longer catch shells. Two buggy hits or one mech/artillery hit wrecks a car; a running mech ram wrecks it, a buggy over 8 m/s dents it. Cars park in rows of 2–4 (`townPlan`).
- Building fires (js/fire.js, `BURN`): `damagePiece` ignites a building panel on any hit of ≥12 raw damage. A destroyed panel lights the panels around the hole; a roof hit lights the nearest top-floor panel. A burning panel grows to full fire, takes `BURN.dps` × `TUNE.bldDmg` damage per second, scorches its instance color, climbs to the panel above every 3 s and spreads sideways every 6 s, until its fuel (50 s) runs out. Spread runs on timers, not randomness. Left alone, one shot burns the two-story test building down in ~37 s (via the normal collapse rule); collapsed burning buildings smolder 30 s. `TUNE.fire` (Tune → Buildings, "Fire ×") scales everything; 0 disables fire. Effects only for the 28 nearest burning panels: persistent flickering flame sprites plus emitted flames, smoke and embers, 3 pooled firelights (pooled so shaders never recompile), crackle when close. At most 160 panels burn at once.
- Weapons (`FIRE`/`RELOAD` in aim.js): buggy is steady and punchy (power 0.9, 0.6 s); mech is rapid fire (power 0.7, 0.2 s, light kick per round); artillery fires heavy shells (power 2.4, 2.4 s). Heavy shells (`sh.heavy`) add `heavyBlast` (shockwave ring, sub-bass thump, longer slow-mo) and **structural hits** (`structHits`/`structHit` in buildings.js). Every building has structural HP = 100 + modules×floors×0.7. A heavy shell does 150 within 4 m of what's left of the building, fading to 0 at 10 m. Below half, the top half of the floors collapses; at 0 the whole building comes down. Small and mid buildings fall to one shell, the biggest towers to two.
- Rendering (js/render.js): `MAP.sky` drives a shader sky (gradient, sun disc + glow, fbm clouds drifting with `time`). It renders raw into a 512 half-float cube map, one face per frame (`skyFaceTick`), and the on-screen dome only samples it and tone-maps. It is also baked once into `sc.environment` (PMREM), so all standard materials get sky reflections and ambient light. Hemisphere light is cut to 30%, exposure 0.92, and `tuneEnv()` sets ordinary materials to envMapIntensity 0.5 (glossy materials opt out via `userData.envTuned`). Shadows: ±60 m around the player on the town (4096 map on High), ±30 on Low. Post on High: half-float scene target → UnrealBloom (fire/explosions/lamps) → gamma → grade (saturation/contrast/tint/vignette in display space, per-map `sky.grade`) → FXAA. **Don't use a multisampled render target** (it corrupted the image on Intel/ANGLE), and keep grading after gamma (contrast in linear 8-bit crushed the shadows to black). Post scripts load from jsdelivr three@0.128.0 examples/js; if missing, High/Medium fall back to Low. Medium = the High pipeline at pixel ratio ≤1 with 2048 shadows. Auto resolution (`autoRes`) drops the pixel ratio 0.25 when frames average >24 ms for 2 s and raises it after 5 s under 17.5 ms, within `PR_CAP` [ceiling, floor] per quality.
- Performance: map-wide instanced things (trees, trunks, rocks, lamps, cars, building panels/slabs) are `TiledInst`: one `InstancedMesh` per 64 m tile with a world-space bounding sphere, so both the camera and the shadow camera cull whole tiles (three r128's `InstancedMesh` turns `frustumCulled` off by default; tiles turn it back on). Same `setMatrixAt/setColorAt` by global index as before; fill, then `build()`. `tileCull(far)` (main loop) also hides tiles wholly beyond the fog, and `terrainLod` does the same for terrain chunks; the camera far plane stays long so foundry beacons still show. With the town fog at 55–205 m this keeps draw calls and triangles at or below what the old 360 m day town drew. On Intel UHD 620 the town is limited by pixel shading, not triangles: pixel ratio matters most, then environment lighting and shadow filtering.
- Materials: `SM()` adds a normal map made from the texture's own brightness (`normalFromCanvas`), so every procedural texture has surface relief. Modern facades get a gloss mask (glass smooth/semi-metallic, walls rough) plus bumps; glass towers envMapIntensity 1.25. Cars are clear-coat MeshPhysicalMaterial with glossy glass. Ground has a bump map. Trees are lumpy displaced-icosahedron canopies with vertex shading (3-blob clusters near, single blobs far). Buildings have merged rooftop detail (`B.roofMesh`: parapet, AC units, tanks, antennas; hidden on collapse). Town ground has painted contact shadows under buildings, cars and trees.
- Chase camera trails the heading; per-mode distance/height in `CAM_R`/`CAM_EL` (artillery pulls back and up). Drag to look around, eases back after 1.5s.
- Swarm foundries (js/swarm.js, `NEST`): `MAP.mission.nests` multi-storey buildings away from the start, spread out, picked by seed from map + run number. A foundry (320 HP) sits on the ground floor: scrap heap, caged glowing core, pistons, exhaust stack through the roof. Marked by a red beacon column (shader, ignores fog, normal blending so it reads against a bright sky), an exhaust plume, scrap plates and blinking red lamps on the facade (instanced, hidden as panels go), a red-brown ground stain (vertex colours via `editGround`, grows as a ragged ring) and a HUD marker with health. Damage: blasts (`nestBlast`, 30% through walls), shells hitting the core, mech bash (95), fire in its building (0.9/s per burning panel), collapse of its building (60% full, 15% top floors). Feeding: `setPieceState` calls `swarmFeed` whenever a piece is destroyed; feed goes to living foundries within 90 m (not their own building), less with distance, ×0.25 if the piece was burning. Growth = feed/160 sets spawn interval 24→7 s, alive cap 1→4, crawler HP +35%, stain 7→24 m, beacon brightness. Units leave by reachable "doors" outside the building; half the crawlers guard their foundry.
- Missions (js/mission.js, `MAP.mission`): phases brief → nests → convoy → extract → over; the game pauses while the overlay is open (Enter deploys). Town: 3 foundries, then 3 evac trucks (300 HP) wait at the east end of the z=22 street and roll west along `convoy.route` once the player is within 35 m, keeping ~13 m apart. A truck stops for an intact blocking piece in its lane (jersey barriers across the street: a yellow "Clear the road" marker), a living enemy ahead or the player (honk). Wrecks slide to the roadside. Once rolling, enemies target the nearest of player/truck (`enemyTarget`; bikes prefer trucks) and the swarm counter-attacks from the map ring. Trucks leaving the map: +25 scrap each; all trucks lost = run lost. Extraction: hold 3 s in the cyan ring (`MAP.mission.extract`). Test field: 1 foundry, then extract at the start. Wrecked rig = run lost.
- Sandbox (`MS.sandbox`, picked with the Mission/Sandbox buttons on the briefing card, saved in localStorage `scrapShifterGame`; applies on deploy): phase 'sandbox', no foundries or convoy. Wanted (js/wanted.js): pieces destroyed by the player (`setPieceState` → `wantedPiece`; enemy shells set `dmgBy='enemy'` in main.js so they don't count) add points by kind (panel 1, slab 2, wall 1.5, shed 3, car 3), kills add crawler 2, bike 1, rig 12. Levels at 8/35/80/150/250 points. 20 s without adding points starts a 3/s drain. Per level, alive at once: L1 2 crawlers 1 bike; L2 4+2; L3 4+2 + 1 rival rig; L4 5+2+2; L5 6+3+3 (spawned from the map ring). The objective panel shows stars, the cool-off timer, points to the next level and time; it flashes on level up. A wreck ends the sandbox: debrief with time survived, peak wanted, kills, rival rigs; all scrap is banked.
- Rival rigs (js/rivals.js, `RIG`): `makeRig` deep-clones the player's `root` (including bolted-on upgrades), maps every part/piv/muz/wheel/blade/bomb by parallel traversal, tints materials red (cached), and adds a red lamp and a HUD marker. They're `enemies` of type 'rig' (300 HP, `e.armor()` per mode like the player's, 1.5× mid-transform), so all weapons, turrets, the drone laser and health bars work on them. `rigSetMode` snapshots the parts and runs the same staggered transform as the player. AI every 0.5 s (min 5 s per mode): >125 m buggy to close in, 60–125 m artillery (high arc without a line of sight, scatter grows with range, warning rings), 20–60 m buggy strafing, <20 m mech (3-round bursts, shield bash 22 within 4.2 m, knocks you back); at wanted 4+ occasional gyro bombing runs (flies 14 m up over roofs, drops when its predicted impact is within 4 m of you, flies on 3 s after a pass, lands to transform when out of bombs, too hot or after 18 s). Mech legs swing procedurally with footstep sounds; a destroyed flying rig falls. Draw cost (`rigLod`, run at the top of `rigTick`): a rig is ~136 meshes, 52 of them parts folded away to nothing in buggy pose. Meshes under `RIG.lodR` (0.45 m) cast no shadow; each frame parts scaled below 0.03 are hidden, meshes under `RIG.lodR` are hidden beyond `RIG.lodD` (70 m), and the whole rig is hidden past the fog. Rotor blades, bombs and the lamp keep their own visibility. Measured on the race grid: ~245 draw calls per nearby rig before, 77 near and 43 far after.
- Salvage and upgrades: kills drop scrap pickups (crawler 10, bike 5; magnet within 8 m); foundry +60, truck +25, win +50. A win banks it all, a loss half. `SAVE` (localStorage `scrapShifterSave`) holds banked scrap, run count and upgrade levels. Garage upgrades (5 levels): armor (+15 max hull), engine (+7% speed), autoloader (−7% reload), cannon (+8% shell power), heat sinks (−12% mech heat). `applyUpgrades` sets `PL_MAX`, `UPG` (drive.js), `RELOAD`, `FIRE` and bolts parts onto the rig (side plates, exhaust stacks, ammo drums, barrel sleeve, cooling fins).
- Race mode (js/race.js, js/raceplan.js; map `race`, "Neon Run", `MS.race`): picked with the Race button on the briefing card, which reloads the page on `?map=race` (Mission/Sandbox there reload on the town). Same neon night look as the town.
  - Track: `RACE_WP` waypoints with rounded corners, resampled every 2 m (`MAP.race.track`: x, z, heading). About 3.3 km: a square spiral of three lanes (190 / 144 / 98 m from the centre, 14 m wide, a few kinks) that ends at the arena's west gate. About 3.2 min flat out, 3.5–4 with fighting. `racePlan` walks the track and puts a building on each side wherever one fits, so the strips between lanes fill with one row (164 buildings, ~8,100 pieces, taller toward the centre); lamps alternate sides; five jersey-barrier slaloms and six rows of parked cars sit on straights.
  - Arena (`RARENA`, `MAP.arena`): 128 m square, walled, one gate. Levels are raised rectangles of the heightfield (`raceHeight`): main deck 60 m square at 3 m with ramps on three sides, a 20 m top deck at 6 m on it with two ramps, two corner bunkers at 2.5 m. `buildArena` (called from structures.js) adds the perimeter, deck-edge and ramp-side walls as indestructible `pieces` (`hp: Infinity`, `fixed`), so movement, shells, sight lines, the camera and the nav grid all respect them; deck walls stand 0.6 m above the deck as a parapet. Cover: scrap walls, jersey barriers, sheds and parked cars on the floor and decks, all destructible.
  - Flow (`RS.phase`, mirrored into `MS.phase`): `grid` (3 s countdown, keys locked) → `race` → `arena` once the player is through the gate. Progress is the nearest track sample searched only in a window around the last one (`trkNear`), so cutting across lanes doesn't count: off the route shows a "Rejoin the route" marker, and the gate pushes back a rig that hasn't driven the route. The first three racers in get 60 / 40 / 20% hull repaired. `RACE.gate` (60 s) after the first arrival the gate closes: rivals still outside are wrecked, a player outside loses. After `RACE.sudden` (150 s) everyone in the arena loses 3 hull/s. Last rig standing wins (+100 scrap). Wrecked on the track, a rig is rebuilt by pit-crew bots over `RACE.back` (2.5 s) and returns with full hull and no ammo (`raceRespawn` for the player, a fresh `raceRig` for a rival, held still by `pitT`/`stun`). The pit animation (`pitStart`/`pitTick`): the parts are blown out around the wreck, hover bots fly in (4 for the player, 2 per rival, pooled), the parts hop back to their buggy pose one after another under welding flashes, sparks and zaps, and the bots fly off. A rig that died off the road is put back on the centreline first; wrecked in the arena, it is out for good.
  - Gun towers (js/towers.js, `TOWER`): 10 scrap lattice towers, one every ~300 m beside the track (`racePlan` reserves the spot first, so buildings and lamps leave a gap; the plinth is a `solids` circle). They're `enemies` of type 'tower' (90 HP, never move), so every weapon, the health bars and the cursor snap work on them. Each picks the nearest racer within 78 m with a clear `fireLine` (player or rival on the track; nobody in the pit or the arena; the player counts as a bit nearer), slews its head at 1.9 rad/s and fires a minigun inside 42 m (6 dps × hit chance, which drops hard with target speed: boosting past costs almost nothing) and a two-grenade salvo every 4.4–5.4 s (power 0.75, low arc, red warning rings, leads short on purpose so a moving racer gets away). Tested: a buggy at 17 m/s in a straight line loses ~6–27 hull per tower, at 22 m/s 0–2, parked in front ~6/s. Its lamp brightens before a salvo. A tower the player wrecks pays `TOWER.bounty` (2) shells and doesn't count as a kill; wrecked towers stay down until the race restarts (`towerReset` from `raceReset`). The minigun sound shares the turrets' loop (`towerMg()` in `turretTick`). HUD: a red 'Gun tower' marker with health when one is ahead within 95 m.
  - Ammo: nobody starts with any. 113 crates in rows every 46 m give `RACE.crate` (2) shells and come back after `RACE.respawn` (2.5 s). `fire()` asks `raceAmmo()`, which spends one shell per shot (buggy or mech). No hull regen once in the arena.
  - Player rules: `setMode` refuses artillery and gyro (their buttons are hidden) and refuses the mech until the player is in the arena (`RS.pArena`); a race always starts as a buggy; the RC bike launches once (`RS.bikeUsed`), never rebuilds, can't take mech form, and the turrets don't deploy while it is driven.
  - Rivals: eight rigs (`RACE.rivals`; `names`, `tint`, `lamp` and `spd` have one entry each) from `makeRig(x,z,{tint,lamp,name})` (own colour, halo and name; 120 HP). Start grid (`MAP.race.grid`): three rows of three, 4 m apart across the track at s = 30 / 22 / 14, the player in the middle of the middle row (`playerS`, `playerLat`); slower rivals start in front. `rigTick` asks race.js: on the track they follow a point ahead on the centreline in their lane via `navWant` (so they path round barriers and rubble), slow for corners, boost in bursts on straights (`RACE.boost` 1.28×, 5 s on / 4 s off), steer for the next crate, pull out to pass, and rubber-band a little on the player (`RACE.spd`, ×1.07 behind, ×1.15 far behind, ×0.92 far ahead). They shoot racers within 55 m but only spend shells above a reserve that grows toward the arena. In the arena they target the nearest racer in it (each other included), fight as a buggy, switch to mech inside 16 m or when out of shells (the bash needs none). Damage scales: rival blasts do `RACE.foeDmg` (0.4) to rivals and `RACE.plDmg` (0.35) to the player; kills only count for the player when `dmgBy==='player'`.
- Gyro mode (js/gyro.js, `GYRO`, key 4): every part has a 4th pose (`po[3]`, the buggy layout by default); tyres lie flat on the stabilizer struts (now horizontal arms) with blades and a blur disc, spun by `gyroTick`; a 4-bomb rack folds out under the hull. Takes off after the transform and holds `GY.alt` (8–26 m, scroll) above the ground, 4 m over any roof under or ~1 s ahead (`roofAt`); no collisions while airborne (`gyroAir()`). WASD: 13 m/s forward, turns in place, tilts into motion. Click drops a bomb (hold for a stick, 0.35 s apart) that keeps 90% of the forward speed; the impact ring and fall arc use `predict()` from `bombLaunch()`. Each drop plays the tail of `sfx/bomb-drop.mp3` cut to the fall time, so the whistle peaks as the bomb lands. Bombs are artillery shells in all but delivery: `FIRE[3]` power 2.4, heavy (structural hits, shockwave); one rack refills a bomb every 3 s (autoloader upgrade shortens it). No self-damage from 8 m up (blast is 3D). Flying builds heat (6/s, `HEAT` shared with the mech, heat sink upgrade applies); at 100 it descends (holding 4 m over roofs until clear), lands and vents (heat only drops once landed), then takes off again at 35. While it vents on the ground the artillery minigun turrets slide out (`mgK` in turrets.js lerps their parts between the tucked and deployed poses) and defend it, then tuck away for take-off. Switching to another mode in the air queues it (`GY.land`): it lands first, then transforms; pressing 4 again cancels. Armor 1.4× (fragile). Crawlers fire flak bursts at it within 46 m (6 per hit); bikes ignore it while it flies; trucks don't stop for it and crawlers don't bump it. The rotor sound is the drone loop pitched down. Camera sits higher and further back (`CAM_R[3]`, `CAM_EL[3]`).
- RC bike (js/rcbike.js, `RCB`, key 5 or the bar button): only from buggy mode. A player-coloured scrap bike (olive, green lamp) is thrown forward off the deck and the keys, camera (`rcCamera`, blended by `RC.k`), shadows and work light move to it; the rig brakes and sits parked (still a target; the drone's rig tag shows where it is and its hull). While the bike is under control the rig's two minigun turrets slide out and defend it exactly as in artillery mode (`guard` in `turretTick`, same `mgK` deploy as the gyro vent), and tuck away when control returns. `rcCtl()` gates the rig: `drive` ignores the keys, `setMode` is blocked, `aimTick` rests the gun, `fire()` goes to `rcFire`. Bike form: 16 m/s, leans, its only weapon is the charge: click/Space detonates it (`RCB.pw` 2.2, not heavy; hurts the rig too if close). While driving it, 3 = mech form and 1 = bike form (0.7 s, same staggered-pose idea: body stands on end, wheels become side-by-side feet, legs and gun arm fold out); the mech walks 5.5 m/s, turns in place, takes 0.7× damage and fires a minigun while click/Space is held: arm follows the cursor (snaps to an enemy by it), 0.3 s spin-up, bullets go down the barrel to 45 m and stop at the first thing `rcTrace` meets: an enemy takes 20 dps, anything else a tiny `applyBlast` per 0.12 s (chews panels, cars, foundries; under the 12-damage fire threshold). It uses the turrets' minigun loop and tail (`turretTick` asks `rcMg()`, pitched 1.1×). 5 again parks the bike and returns to the rig; 5 from the buggy within `RCB.range` (170 m, also its leash) takes it over again. 45 HP: enemies target whichever of rig/bike is nearer (`enemyTarget` → `RCT`), enemy shells hit it (`rcShotHit`), blasts hurt it (`rcBlast`); destroyed, its charge goes off at 70%. Detonated or destroyed, it rebuilds in 12 s. HUD: Bike pill (Ready / form, hull, distance / Parked / Rebuilding).

## Design decisions so far

- Destruction approach: swap-state buildings plus crater terrain, not full physics destruction (browser performance and future multiplayer sync).
- Keep destruction deterministic so multiplayer can be added later without a rewrite.
- Transforming should take time and leave you briefly exposed, so it's a real decision.
- The mech should look like its own scrap-built thing, not a copy of a famous car-robot franchise.
- Joe has used Babylon.js on other projects. The prototype uses three.js; switching engines is still open.
- **The current 120 m field is a test map only.** The real battle map will be much bigger, with a destructible town. Don't tune layouts, spawns or balance to the test field as if final. Keep systems map-agnostic: structures, spawning, AI and waves should take their layout from map data rather than hard-coded positions. Expect the bigger map to need chunked terrain (crater updates touch only nearby tiles), multi-piece buildings that can collapse floor by floor (still swap-state and deterministic), and grid pathfinding for AI in streets.
